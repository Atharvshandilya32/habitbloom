# Product Experiment Log

This file tracks controlled product experiments aimed at improving activation, retention, and organic growth.

## Experiment 1: Habit Completion UX
* **Experiment**: Add onboarding tooltip and increase hit area for first habit completion.
* **Date**: 2026-08-12
* **Hypothesis**: Users are failing to complete their first habit because the checkbox is too small or non-obvious. A tooltip and larger hit area will increase the Day-1 Activation Rate.
* **Change**: 
  - Increased padding on the habit completion checkbox.
  - Added a one-time "Click here to complete" tooltip for a user's first habit.
* **Target Users**: All new signups (August 15 Cohort vs previous cohorts).
* **Metric**: First-Session Habit Completion Rate; Day-1 Activation Rate.

## Experiment 2: Progressive Disclosure
* **Experiment**: Hide advanced features (Insights, Goals, Spaces) for new users.
* **Date**: 2026-08-12
* **Hypothesis**: Showing too many features immediately causes cognitive overload, reducing Day-1 retention. Limiting the initial view to Today's Habits, Progress, and Garden will improve focus and retention.
* **Change**: 
  - Hid 'Insights', 'Goals', and 'Spaces' tabs for users with Level < 3.
  - Simplified bottom navigation for new users.
* **Target Users**: All new signups.
* **Metric**: Day-1 to Day-7 Retention Rate.
* **Result**: TBD
* **Decision**: TBD

## Experiment 3: Shareable Progress Cards
* **Experiment**: Introduce lightweight, privacy-preserving sharing of milestones.
* **Date**: 2026-08-12
* **Hypothesis**: Highly engaged users want to share their progress but lack an easy, attractive way to do so without exposing private habit names. A shareable card will increase organic referral traffic.
* **Change**: 
  - Added a "Share Milestone" button when a user reaches a streak of 7 days or levels up.
  - Generates an image with Consistency, Best Streak, and XP.
* **Target Users**: Users who reach Level 5+ or a 7-day streak.
* **Metric**: Number of cards generated; Number of organic signups from shared links.
