# Monetization Experiments

## Experiment 1: Phase 12 Premium Wedge Validation (Advanced Growth Insights)

### Overview
This experiment introduces the "Advanced Growth Insights" feature to a controlled Early Access group to test the premium hypothesis established in Phase 10.

### Target Group
- **Size:** 5–10 users.
- **Criteria:** Active users with at least 60 days of habit data to ensure insights populate effectively.

### Hypothesis
Users who receive deeper, evidence-backed views of their long-term habit patterns will find HabitBloom more valuable and will return to the product more often. 

### Metrics to Track
1. **Activation:** Did the user open the premium feature? (`premium_feature_opened`)
2. **Engagement:** Did they interact with it? (Changing periods to 14/30/60/90 days).
3. **Repeat Usage:** Do they return to the dashboard over multiple sessions?
4. **Value:** What is the feedback rating? (`premium_feedback_submitted`)

### Qualitative Questions for Follow-up (Manual)
- Which insight was most useful?
- Which insight was least useful?
- Did the long-term comparisons help you understand your habits?
- Would you come back to this dashboard?
- What information is missing?
- Would you miss this feature if it disappeared?
- Would this be valuable enough to pay for in 2027?
